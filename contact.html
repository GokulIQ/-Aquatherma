Emergency Action Protocol

What to Do Right Now While Waiting for Dispatch

Follow these 3 crucial steps to stop active water damage while your technician is en route:

1

Shut Off Cold Supply

Turn the valve located on the pipe directly above the heater clockwise (or quarter-turn for ball valves).

2

Cut Fuel / Power

For gas: switch thermostat dial to 'OFF'. For electric: switch off dedicated 30-amp circuit breaker.

3

Open Hot Faucet

Open a hot faucet in the lowest sink to relieve residual hydrostatic pressure from the hot water lines.

Before You Get in Touch

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about booking, estimates, and contacting our team.

How do I request a service appointment?

Complete the service booking form with your contact information, the service you need, and your preferred date. Contact our team to confirm availability and your appointment time.

Who should I contact for an urgent water heater problem?

For an urgent repair request, call our emergency dispatch line at (800) 555-4689. Share your location and describe the problem so the team can discuss availability and the next steps.

What information should I include in my request?

Include your name, phone number, email, ZIP code, and a brief description of the issue. If available, add the heater's model, approximate age, fuel type, and any error code shown on its display.

Can I get an estimate before booking?

Visit our Pricing Guide for sample package prices and to request an estimate. Final pricing depends on the equipment, existing connections, and installation conditions confirmed during an on-site assessment.

How can I confirm that you serve my area?

Use the ZIP code checker on this page to view your area, then call dispatch to confirm service availability and the expected arrival window for your address.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Call (800) 555-4689 with the name and phone number used for your booking. Let the team know your scheduled date and any preferred alternative so they can help update your appointment.
