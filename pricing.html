What Goes Into Your Final Estimate?

Every home is built differently. Here is why estimates can vary and how our certified plumbers determine exact, transparent pricing before turning a single wrench.

* Percentages reflect representative averages for residential retrofits. We provide fully line-itemed written quotes before any work begins.

1. System Type & Storage Capacity

Standard 40–50 gallon storage cylinders cost less initially than high-output 80-gallon commercial tanks or multi-burner condensing tankless systems with continuous flow.

2. Energy Efficiency & Heat Exchangers

Condensing units and hybrid heat pump systems utilize dual stainless steel exchangers or micro-compressors with 0.96+ UEF ratings, resulting in higher initial equipment cost but lower fuel bills.

3. Installation Access & Rigging

An open basement swap takes fewer labor hours than extracting a waterlogged 200 lb tank from a tight finished attic, narrow crawlspace, or third-floor apartment utility closet.

4. Plumbing, Gas & Electrical Retrofits

Converting from tank to tankless often requires upgrading from 1/2" to 3/4" gas piping, while hybrid heat pumps require a dedicated 240V breaker and condensate drainage routing.

5. Local Building Permits & Safety Codes

Municipal building codes mandate thermal expansion tanks on closed water loops, seismic strapping kits in earthquake zones, and official municipal safety permits (₹65–₹220).

6. Old Equipment Disposal & Recycling

AquaTherma handles the draining, safe disconnection, heavy transport, and EPA-certified metal scrap recycling of your decommissioned water heater so nothing sits on your curb.
