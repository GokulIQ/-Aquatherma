High-Efficiency Condensing Tankless System

Heats water instantaneously on demand through dual stainless steel heat exchangers, providing continuous hot water without a storage reservoir.

How It Works

Cold water flows past a digital sensor that instantly ignites high-output burners, heating water as it circulates through commercial heat exchangers.

Space Requirements

Ultra-compact suitcase footprint mounts cleanly to utility walls, freeing up 100% of basement or closet floor space.

Energy Efficiency

Ultra-high efficiency up to 0.96 UEF. Zero standby energy loss, reducing household heating fuel usage by up to 30%.

Hot Water Supply

Continuous, endless hot water delivery at 8.0–11.0 GPM. Never runs out during simultaneous showers, dishwashing, and laundry.

Installation Factors

Requires dedicated 3/4" gas supply line or 120A electrical subpanel service, with direct concentric exterior PVC/polypropylene venting.

Maintenance Needs

Annual descaling flush using food-grade citric acid solution to dissolve calcium scale and maintain heat exchanger warranty.
