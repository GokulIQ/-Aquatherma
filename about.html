Our Core Philosophy

Why Water Heating Demands Pure Specialization

A residential water heater is the only appliance in your home that combines 60 PSI high hydrostatic water pressure with either high-voltage electricity (240V) or explosive natural gas and carbon monoxide combustion.

When an inexperienced handyman installs a water heater without a thermal expansion tank, improper gas sediment traps, or insufficient vent pitch, the consequences range from premature tank rupture to dangerous carbon monoxide backdrafting.

Code & Safety Guarantee

Our 6-Point Precision Safety Audit

Before marking any water heater installation complete, our technicians verify and photograph every step on our standardized digital inspection checklist:

  • Thermal Expansion Tank: Pressure-matched to municipal static PSI.
  • Temperature & Pressure Relief: Tested with code-compliant gravity drop pipe.
  • Gas Line & Sediment Trap: Tested with electronic sniffer and bubble solution.
  • Combustion Exhaust Draft: Verified chimney or power-vent flue updraft.
  • Dielectric Unions: Installed on copper joints to prevent galvanic corrosion.
  • Containment Drain Pan: Elevated with piped runoff to floor drain.
