Case Study Overview

Project Context & Scope of Work

The homeowners in Westfield Estates were frustrated by cold showers every morning as their family grew. Their 14-year-old atmospheric gas tank required nearly an hour to recover and had begun showing mineral weeping at the base fittings. AquaTherma engineered a complete conversion to an ultra-efficient wall-hung condensing tankless unit.

Scope of Service: Complete system decommissioning, utility line upgrading, mechanical wall-mounting, code-compliant venting, and high-flow commissioning.
Property Setting: Single-family residence, multi-bath simultaneous demand requirement.

Diagnostic Phase

The Initial Problem & Technical Assessment

Detailed pre-service diagnostic inspection and root-cause analysis conducted by AquaTherma certified field technicians.

A family of five suffered from severe hot water exhaustion during morning peak hours. The existing tank suffered from heavy lime buildup, inadequate combustion air, and occupied valuable floor space in their finished basement utility room.

Engineering Execution

Engineered Solution & Work Performed

Step-by-step technical execution following local plumbing code, manufacturer specifications, and safety protocols.

Technical Scope of Work

Our technicians removed the obsolete 50-gallon tank, upgraded the gas supply line to 3/4-inch black iron from the primary manifold, and mounted an Energy Star condensing tankless unit. We installed dual concentric PVC venting through the rim joist, built copper supply headers with lead-free brass ball valves, and integrated a webstone isolation valve flush kit for effortless maintenance.

The family now enjoys continuous, limitless hot water across three simultaneous showers. The wall-mounted footprint reclaimed 14 square feet of floor space, while the 0.96 UEF condensing unit delivered an estimated 28% reduction in monthly water-heating gas usage.
