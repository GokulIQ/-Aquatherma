Master Plumber Safety Caution:

Never tamper with or cap the temperature and pressure (T&P) relief valve if steam or scalding water is continuously discharging. Turn off the cold water inlet valve immediately and contact a licensed master plumber. High static pressure inside an unvented water heater can pose severe vessel rupture hazards.
