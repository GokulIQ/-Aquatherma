Phase 1: Hydronic Inflow Detection

Turbine Flow Sensor Activation

When any hot water faucet or fixture is opened anywhere in the home, cold water begins moving through the intake port. A high-precision digital magnetic turbine detects water movement as low as 0.4 gallons per minute, signaling the central PCB processor within milliseconds.

Minimum Flow Threshold: 0.4 GPM activation (prevents false firing from pipe pressure fluctuations).
Sensor Technology: Dual Hall-effect magnetic pulse turbine with instant pulse-width modulation output.
Pre-Ignition Check: Electronic thermistors instantly measure incoming groundwater temp to calculate precise burner firing rate.
Retrofit & Compatibility Architecture

Built Around Your Home's Heating Needs.

Transitioning from a cylindrical storage tank to high-performance tankless requires engineering harmony across four residential utilities. AquaTherma validates every parameter on-site to ensure optimal performance.

Pillar 01 — Fuel Delivery

Gas Supply Capacity & Line Sizing

Tankless systems demand between 160,000 to 199,000 BTU/hr when heating multiple fixtures simultaneously. Our master plumbers measure your utility meter capacity and run dedicated 3/4" or negative-pressure 1/2" gas piping with digital manometer drop-testing to eliminate burner starvation.

Up to 199k BTU/hr Support 3.5" to 10.5" W.C. Pressure Natural Gas & Propane Kits
Pillar 02 — Circuit & Safety

Electrical Service & Smart Diagnostics

Modern condensing tankless heaters eliminate standing pilot flames in favor of electronic direct-spark ignition and variable-speed combustion fans. We install a dedicated 120V 60Hz GFCI circuit with surge mitigation, protecting sensitive microprocessors, freeze-prevention heat tape, and Wi-Fi modules.

Dedicated 120V / 15A Outlet <3A Operating Power Automatic Freeze Protection
Pillar 03 — Flue & Drainage

Direct Sidewall Venting & Condensate Drainage

Because condensing units extract nearly all heat from combustion flue gases, exhaust temperatures stay cool enough for lightweight Schedule 40 PVC or Polypropylene pipes running straight through an exterior sidewall. Acidic moisture is safely routed through a limestone neutralizer capsule.

Concentric PVC / Polypro Zero Masonry Chimney Needed Inline Acid Neutralizer
Pillar 04 — Longevity

Hard Water Protection & Service Isolation Valves

To safeguard the 15-year factory warranty, every AquaTherma tankless install includes heavy-duty brass service isolation valves with integrated purge ports. This enables 45-minute annual descaling flushes without cutting pipes or disrupting your domestic cold water supply.

Full-Port Brass Webstone Valves Inline Scale-Inhibitor Filter Quick 45-Min Descaling Port
