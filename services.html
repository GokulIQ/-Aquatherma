Self-Assessment Guide

What's Wrong With Your Water Heater?

Identify your water heater symptoms to understand the probable cause and appropriate technical solution.

Popping or Rumbling

Likely Cause: Heavy mineral scale trapped at the bottom boiling into steam pockets.

Fix: Descaling Flush

Water Is Freezing Cold

Likely Cause: Burned-out heating element, tripped high-limit switch, or extinguished pilot light.

Fix: Diagnostic Inspection

Rusty Brown Hot Water

Likely Cause: Depleted sacrificial anode rod causing internal steel vessel oxidation.

Fix: Anode Swap or Replace

Puddle Beneath Tank

Likely Cause: Dripping T&P valve, cracked drain valve, or outer vessel weld breach.

Fix: Urgent Leak Repair
