Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AquaTherma Water Heating Specialists LLC ("AquaTherma", "we", "our") handles customer information when you use our website or schedule residential water heater services.

1. Information We Collect

When requesting an estimate or booking emergency services, we collect contact details including your full name, physical service address, telephone number, email, and technical specifications regarding your existing water heating equipment.

2. How Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to dispatch licensed technicians to your property, coordinate appointment windows, provide upfront estimates, process manufacturer warranty paperwork, and maintain service records.

3. Non-Disclosure & Security

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers or lead generation brokers.

4. Template Demonstration Notice

This website is currently delivered as a frontend HTML template for commercial presentation. All forms simulate client-side validation without transmitting real customer records to a production database.
